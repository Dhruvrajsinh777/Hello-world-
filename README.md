# Kurukshetra: Dharma Yuddha ⚔️

A turn-based battle game set in the world of the **Mahabharata**.

Choose a Pandava warrior and fight your way through six great Kaurava champions
on the field of Kurukshetra to restore dharma.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in any web browser. There's nothing to install.

- Pick your hero: **Arjuna** 🏹, **Bhima** 💪, **Yudhishthira** ⚖️ or **Abhimanyu** ⚔️
- Each turn, use one of 4 moves (click it or press keys `1`–`4`)
- Moves cost **Prana** ✨, which regenerates every turn; powerful astras also have cooldowns
- Status effects: 🔥 Fury (+50% damage), 🥀 Weak (-30% damage), ♨️ Burn, 💫 Stun, 🛡️ Shield
- After each victory you recover half your lost HP and choose a **boon from the gods**

## The campaign

1. 😈 **Dushasana**: the insulter of Draupadi
2. 🛡️ **Jayadratha**: protected by Shiva's boon
3. 🧔 **Bhishma**: the grandsire who heals himself with *Iccha Mrityu*
4. 📿 **Drona**: guru of the Kurus and his *Chakravyuha*
5. ☀️ **Karna**: son of Surya, who unleashes *Vasavi Shakti* when wounded
6. 👑 **Duryodhana**: the final duel, with his iron body and *Last Stand*
